How much does a website cost in Kenya?

Landing pages start at KES 10,000 and basic websites at KES 15,000. SEO websites are KES 40,000 to 80,000, school and e-commerce websites start at KES 50,000, and learning management systems are KES 80,000 to 120,000. Logos are KES 1,000.

What is an SEO website?

It is a website built so Google can understand it and show it to people searching in Nairobi and across Kenya. That includes fast loading, clean structure, keyword-focused pages and local setup.

What is a learning management system (LMS)?

An LMS lets you sell and run online courses with lessons, quizzes, certificates, student dashboards and payments. It suits trainers, schools and companies.

Can you build a website for my school or institution?

Yes. We build institution websites with admissions, programs, news and an optional student portal. Request a quote and we will price it to your needs.

How do I pay?

Prices are in Kenyan shillings. We agree the quote first and share payment details on WhatsApp.
