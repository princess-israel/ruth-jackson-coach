Make your videos for free

A practical playbook for creating course lessons and marketing clips for your TUI programs, without paying for any tool or subscription. Every option here has a genuinely free path.

Total cost  KES 0 / $0
The one honest rule

No tool gives away unlimited, high quality, AI-generated video for free, because it runs on costly machines. So the free path splits in two: record your own lessons (free forever, and better for teaching), and use free daily credits on AI tools for short promo clips. Pick the lane that fits what you are making.

Choose your lane
For teaching · free forever

Record your lessons

Screen shares, talking-to-camera, narrated slides. No credits, no limits, no watermark.

OBS Studio no watermark Record your screen and webcam together. Best for software demos and slide lessons.
CapCut free editor Trim, add auto-captions, music and your logo. The all-rounder editor, on phone or desktop.
Canva (free plan) templates Slide-style lessons and branded intros with your colours saved in a brand kit.
Your phone already yours A modern phone shoots crisp 1080p. Face a window for light and it looks professional.
Make it look hired, not homemade
  • Light: face a window. Daylight beats any paid ring light.
  • Sound first: earphone mic or a clip mic. Clear audio matters more than 4K.
  • Script it: read from notes taped beside the lens so your eyes stay up.
  • Shoot 16:9 for the course player; keep each clip short and focused.
For marketing · free daily credits

Generate promo clips

Short AI clips for reels, adverts and course intros. Free tiers give you a set of credits each day; expect a small watermark you can cover in CapCut.

Google AI Studio (Veo) limited free Highest quality and adds sound. A few free generations to start.
Kling AI daily credits Free credits refresh each day. Strong, realistic results from text or a photo.
Hailuo / MiniMax daily credits Free daily credits with especially natural, lifelike motion.
Luma · Pika · Runway free tier Each gives a monthly free allowance. Rotate between them for more free clips.
Turn a free clip into a branded one
  • Generate on the tool's own website, then download the clip.
  • Drop it into CapCut, add your logo, captions and music.
  • Your logo and a caption bar cover the free-tier watermark.
  • Free amounts change often, so treat them as a bonus, not a promise.

Keep it simple

Your weekly rhythm

Script it

Ten minutes. Three points and a call to action. Keep it to what one clip needs.

Record or generate

Lesson? Record yourself (teaching lane). Promo? Spend a free credit (marketing lane).

Edit and caption

CapCut: trim, auto-caption, add your logo and a free music bed.

Export two shapes

16:9 for the course and site, 9:16 for status and reels, from the same edit.

Post and link back

End every clip pointing to coachruthjackson.com so views turn into enrolments.

On your website

The built-in studio (optional, later)

Your site has an AI Video Studio built in, but it is switched off and costs nothing while it sits there. It only ever charges you if you choose to add a pay-as-you-go key and press Generate, at a few cents per clip. Nothing in this playbook needs it. Leave it off, use the free lanes above, and switch it on only if one day the convenience is worth a small per-clip cost.
