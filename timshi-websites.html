The steps

How do you create a website?

Five simple steps from idea to a live site.

  • 1Decide the goal. Do you want calls, bookings, sales or sign-ups? Every page should push towards one action.
  • 2Choose a domain and hosting. Pick a short, memorable name and a reliable host with good support.
  • 3Plan your pages. Home, About, Services or Products, and Contact is enough to start.
  • 4Write and design. Use clear words, strong photos and your brand colours. Keep it clean and fast.
  • 5Launch and promote. Publish, share on WhatsApp and social media, and keep improving it.
