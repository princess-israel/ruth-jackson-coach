## Facebook

Many business owners think they need thousands more followers before they can sell. Often the people you need are already following you.

Here are 6 steps I teach to help turn followers into customers:

1. Know your followers: check who follows you and focus on the people who could buy, not just other creators.
2. Post with a plan: use a simple content calendar and strong hooks. Plan your week in one sitting.
3. Run small ads: start with a small, targeted Meta or TikTok budget aimed at people near you.
4. Be found on search: use local keywords like "your service + Nairobi" in your bio and captions.
5. Track what sells: count messages, clicks and orders, not just likes. Repeat what works.
6. Use AI tools that work: try ChatGPT. Ask it to plan 7 days of Instagram posts for your business, then add your own voice and offer before you post.
