## LinkedIn

Most teams adopt AI tools faster than they train people to use them. The result: hours lost on rework, wrong answers and data in the wrong places.

Here are 4 fixes any professional or team can use today (full carousel above):

1. Start with one task. Pick one job you repeat, like drafting emails or summarising notes, and master it before you add more.
2. Give clear instructions. Tell the AI who will read the output, what you need and how long it should be. Clear input gets better output.
3. Always check the answer. AI can sound sure and still be wrong, so check facts, names and numbers before anything is shared.
4. Protect private data. Never paste client details or passwords into an AI tool, and set your team's rules first.

The takeaway for leaders: the tool is the easy part. Training your people to use it clearly, carefully and safely is what turns AI into saved time.
