## Facebook

Two people ask the same AI tool the same question. One gets a vague answer. The other gets something they can use straight away.

The difference is the prompt.

Here are the 5 parts I teach every small business owner and customer care team in Nairobi and across Africa:

1. Role: tell the AI who it should act as
2. Goal: say clearly what you want out
3. Context: explain your business and your city
4. Format: ask for a list, a table or a short reply
5. Tone: choose warm, friendly or formal
