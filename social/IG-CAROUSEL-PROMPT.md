# Instagram Carousel Prompt (Coach Ruth Jackson)

Copy and paste the prompt below, then fill in the topic and photo.

---

Create an Instagram carousel slide for Coach Ruth Jackson in my house style.

**Topic:** [e.g. customer service, AI, digital marketing, cyber security]
**Slide:** [hook only / slides 2 to 7 / full carousel]
**Background photo:** [attached / none]

**Size and format**
- 1080 x 1350 px (Instagram portrait), exported as PNG.
- Save the design file and PNG in `social/<topic>-carousel/`.

**Colours**
- Navy blue `#0B1F3A` (main background and text boxes).
- Bright gold `#FFC933` (labels, key words, lines, frame, corners).
- White `#FFFFFF` (headline and main text).

**Background photo (when I attach one)**
- Remove any old text, names, quote marks or website links from the photo.
- Place the photo in the lower part of the slide and fade the top into solid navy so the headline is easy to read.
- Let the photo show through; do not cover it all in navy.
- If the photo has a moon, sun or bright focal point, move it to the top right corner with a soft golden glow, and keep it clear of the headline.

**Layout (hook slide)**
1. Top left: gold label with navy text, uppercase, wide letter spacing (the topic, e.g. "CUSTOMER SERVICE").
2. Big bold white serif headline (Playfair Display style), 4 lines max, with one key word in gold italics.
3. Short gold line under the headline.
4. Subtitle in a navy box with a gold bar on the left. White bold text, with the emotional phrase in gold.
5. Bottom left: "COACH RUTH JACKSON" on a white rounded label with navy text.
6. Bottom right: "SWIPE →" button, navy with a gold border and gold text.
7. Thin gold frame around the slide with thicker gold corners (top left and bottom right).

**Inner slides (2 onwards)**
- Same colours, frame and fonts.
- Gold slide number (02, 03...) top right, one point per slide, short punchy text.
- Last slide: call to action pointing to my courses at coachruthjackson.com and WhatsApp +254729384374.

**Writing style**
- Hook must stop the scroll: challenge a common belief or name a hidden mistake.
- Speak to business owners and customer care teams.
- Short sentences, simple words, no em-dashes.

**After designing**
- Show me the image, then commit and push it to the working branch (never `main`, so the website is not affected).
