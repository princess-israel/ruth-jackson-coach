Insight: The biggest gain from AI comes from clear instructions, not clever tools.

- Start with one task you repeat, like drafting emails or summarising notes
- Tell the AI who will read it, what you need and how long it should be
- Check facts, names and numbers before you share anything
- Never paste client details or passwords into an AI tool
