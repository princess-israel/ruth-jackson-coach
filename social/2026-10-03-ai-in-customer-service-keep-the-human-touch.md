## LinkedIn

AI is now answering your customers. The question is whether it is helping or quietly hurting your brand.

Across customer care centres, the pattern is clear. AI works best on predictable, repeated questions and on background tasks like ticket summaries and drafting replies. It works worst when a customer is upset, when money or privacy is involved, or when nobody can reach a person.

The teams that get value share three habits:
1. They write approved answers before automating anything.
2. They define exactly when a human takes over.
3. They train agents to review and edit AI drafts, not just send them.

The takeaway for leaders: the tool is the easy part. Training your people to use it with empathy and accuracy is what protects customer trust.
