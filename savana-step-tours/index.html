FAQ

Before You Book

When is the best time to travel?

The dry months, roughly June to October, give the best game viewing and the Great Migration river crossings. Green season is quieter and more affordable.

Do I need a visa or vaccinations?

Most visitors need an eTA or visa for Kenya and a visa for Tanzania. Yellow fever and malaria advice varies by traveller. Check official sources and your doctor before you go.

Can I customise my itinerary?

Yes. Every safari is built around you: pace, budget, interests and group size. Send an enquiry and we will shape a plan.

Is it safe?

We use well-maintained 4x4 vehicles, trained drivers and guides, and keep in contact with our ground team throughout your trip.
